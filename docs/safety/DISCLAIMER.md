# Research prototype only. Not a medical device.

This harness helps explore biomedical engineering ideas and 3D-printable prototypes.
It does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or FDA-cleared designs.

Mandatory:
- Human approves problem, spec, and STL before any print/handling.
- No implantable, invasive, sterile, diagnostic, or dosing builds without qualified review.
- Materials here (PLA/PETG/TPU) are for external, non-sterile prototypes only.
- Label every print: date, material lot, settings, "NOT FOR CLINICAL USE".
